Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What this site is

Stack Tools Lab is a static website. It has no user accounts, no comments and no checkout. Reading articles does not require you to give us any personal information, and this site does not set cookies of its own.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may process request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver pages and protect the service, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not receive or store per-visitor logs from the host.

Contact form

Inquiries are handled through a Google Form linked from the contact page. Anything you type into that form is processed by Google under Google's privacy policy and delivered to the operator. We use it only to reply to your inquiry, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties for marketing.

Outbound and affiliate links

Articles link to vendors' official pages. Some of those links may be affiliate links; when they are, the destination site may set cookies or use similar technology to attribute a sign-up, under that site's own privacy policy. Articles containing affiliate links say so on the page, per our disclosure policy.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example, if analytics are introduced — the change will be recorded here with a new date before it takes effect.
