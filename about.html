How figures are verified

Every number on this site passes the same procedure before publication:

1. Primary sources onlyFigures are read from the vendor's official pricing page, help center, affiliate program page or terms — never from other review sites, aggregators or social posts.
2. Definitions travel with the numbersA limit is published together with what the vendor counts (for example, confirmed subscribers vs. stored contacts) and the billing condition it belongs to (monthly vs. annual billing, audience tier).
3. Check dates are shownEach article carries the date its figures were checked. Pricing changes; when a vendor changes a figure, the article is updated and the update is logged on the page.
4. Unconfirmed figures are omittedIf a price or limit cannot be confirmed on an official page, it does not appear — not as an estimate, not as a rumor.
5. No invented experienceWe do not describe tools we have not used as if we had, and we do not publish usage impressions, benchmark results or testimonials we cannot stand behind. Articles are checks of published terms, and they are labeled as such.

Affiliate disclosure

This site is operated as a business and may earn commissions when readers sign up for tools through links on this site, at no additional cost to the reader. The rules we apply:

  • Articles that contain affiliate links state so on the page.
  • Commissions never change a figure. Prices and limits are reported as published, whether or not a program exists for the tool.
  • Tools without an affiliate program are covered on the same terms as tools with one, when the comparison needs them.
  • No vendor pays for placement, ranking or wording on this site, and sponsored content is not published.

Corrections

If you find a figure that no longer matches a vendor's official page, tell us via the contact form. Corrections are made to the article and recorded in its update log with the new check date.
